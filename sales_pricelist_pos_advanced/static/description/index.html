Advanced Sales Pricelist – Point of Sale bridge

The Point of Sale companion of Advanced Sales Pricelist. It makes the till charge the same prices as your quotations: percentage extra fees work in the POS, and carton or dozen prices are never charged on single units.

Why this module

The Odoo 19 Point of Sale does not ask the server for prices: it downloads the pricelist rules when it loads its data and computes every price in the browser, with its own copy of the pricing logic.

Advanced Sales Pricelist + POS, without this bridge

  • The POS ignores the Unit / Packaging condition: “Box of 5 = 275.00” would be charged for one unit.
  • Same for a price per dozen: it would be charged per unit.
  • The POS ignores Extra Fee (%): cost + 10 % is charged as cost only.
  • The till and the quotation give different prices for the same pricelist.

With this bridge

  • Unit / packaging rules are not sent to the POS: single units keep their unit price.
  • The POS always sells in the product’s unit; a packaging barcode adds its quantity in units.
  • Extra Fee (%) is added by the POS, on the base or the discounted price.
  • Every rule the POS receives is priced exactly like the backend.

How it works

Nothing to configure: assign your pricelists to the shop as usual.

1
Install
Install Advanced Sales Pricelist and Point of Sale; this bridge follows automatically.
2
Assign
In Point of Sale › Configuration › Settings, enable Flexible Pricelists and pick the pricelists the cashier can use.
3
Open
When the session opens, the POS receives only the rules it can apply, with their percentage fees.
4
Scan
Each scan reprices the line, including quantity tiers and the percentage fee.

Features

Screenshots use a demo shop: Arabica Coffee 1kg with Sales Price 100.00 and Cost 50.00, sold by the unit or in a Box of 5, and a Members pricelist assigned to the POS shop.

Setup

One pricelist, two kinds of rules

The Members pricelist has two formula rules, both Cost + 10 % Extra Fee (%). The Box of 5 rule prices the box on quotations and sales orders. The rule with Min. Quantity 5 has no packaging condition: it is the one the POS uses.

Feature 1

Percentage extra fee at the till

Scanning the coffee once charges the Sales Price (100.00): the 5-unit tier is not reached. On the 5th scan the line switches to Cost + 10 % = 55.00 per unit, 275.00 in total, the same amount as a Box of 5 on a quotation.

Same formula as the backend: discount → rounding → fixed fee + percentage fee → min/max margins. The fee can be on the Base Price or the Discounted Price, and also works on rules based on another pricelist. Rules without a percentage fee are still computed by Odoo’s own POS code.
Feature 2

No carton price on a single unit

The Odoo 19 POS always sells in the product’s unit: scanning a packaging barcode adds its quantity in units. Rules with a Unit / Packaging condition are therefore filtered out when the session loads, and for products the POS fetches from the server later. They keep working on quotations and sales orders.

Tip – box price at the till: to give the box price to customers who scan 5 units, add the same formula as a rule without packaging and with Min. Quantity = 5 (as in the screenshots). From the 5th unit, every unit on the line gets the box price.

Built for real-world corner cases

Install order
Point of Sale before or after the main app: the bridge is installed as soon as both are present, and removed with either of them.
Fee after rounding
Sales Price 100, −7 %, rounding 5.00, +10 % on the discounted price = 104.50 in the POS and on quotations.
Margins
The maximum margin caps the fee: +50 % with a maximum margin of 20.00 gives 120.00, not 150.00.
Chained pricelists
A rule based on another pricelist takes that pricelist’s POS price, then adds its own fee (95.00 + 5 % = 99.75).
Products loaded later
Products the POS fetches from the server after opening (e.g. found with the search) get the same filtered rules.
Standard POS untouched
Rules without a percentage fee use Odoo’s own code; Odoo’s 135 POS browser tests pass with the bridge installed.
