Why this module

Distributors sell the same product by the unit, by the dozen and by the carton, at different prices. Standard Odoo 18 pricelists only know the product’s default unit.

In standard Odoo 18

  • A rule cannot target a Unit of Measure: a dozen is always 12 × the unit price.
  • Packagings only round quantities; a carton cannot have its own price.
  • Formula rules accept a fixed extra fee only.
  • Changing the line UoM keeps the number: 24 Units become 24 Dozens.
  • Selecting a packaging only shows a warning when the quantity is not a whole number of packages.
  • Only a basic import template for pricelists; no template for pricelist rules, UoM or packaging prices.

With this module

  • Rules per Unit of Measure: set the real price of a dozen, with its own minimum quantity.
  • Rules per Packaging: price per carton, tiers counted in cartons, formulas computed per carton.
  • Extra Fee (%) next to the fixed fee, on the base or the discounted price.
  • The quantity is converted: 24 Units become 2 Dozens (company setting).
  • The quantity is rounded up to whole packages with a clear notification (company setting).
  • 4 Excel templates with drop-downs and bilingual instructions, offered on the import screen.

How it works

No new workflow: the module extends the pricelist rules you already use.

1
Enable
Turn on Pricelists, Units of Measure and/or Product Packagings in Sales settings.
2
Define
Add rules with a Unit of Measure or a Packaging, by hand or from an Excel template.
3
Sell
On quotations, pick the UoM or packaging: the matching rule is applied automatically.
4
Fall back
Lines without a matching UoM or packaging rule keep the standard product pricing.

Features

Screenshots use a demo beverage distributor (company currency USD): Water Bottle 1.5L sold by the unit, the dozen, the Pack of 6 and the Carton of 24, with Retail, Wholesale and Export Europe (EUR) pricelists.

Menus

Every rule of every pricelist in one place

A new menu Sales › Products › Pricelist Rules sits next to Pricelists. The two options of the module are in Sales › Configuration › Settings › Pricing.

Feature 1

Price per dozen and per carton on the pricelist

The Price Rules tab of each pricelist gets Unit of Measure and Packaging columns, and the rule name says which unit it is for. The Price is then per dozen or per carton, and Min. Quantity counts dozens or cartons: here 25.00 per Carton of 24, and 24.00 from 10 cartons.

Feature 2

UoM and packaging conditions on every rule type

In the Odoo 18 rule form, Unit of Measure and Packaging sit right under the product. Every rule (fixed price, discount or formula) can be limited to a Unit of Measure or a Packaging. A UoM rule can apply to all products or a category, e.g. “4 % off everything sold by the dozen”. A notice on the rule explains in which unit its amounts are expressed.

Feature 3

Percentage extra fee on formula rules

Formula rules get Extra Fee (%) and % on the Base Price / Discounted Price, next to Odoo’s fixed Extra Fee. The formula tip shows a live example. Odoo 18 shows the discount of a Cost based rule as a Markup; the fees work the same way. On a packaging rule the whole formula runs per package: here Cost of a carton + 0.50 + 20 %, with a minimum margin of 1.00.

Order of the formula (as in Odoo): discount → rounding → fixed fee + percentage fee → min/max margins.
Example: Sales Price 100.00, discount 10 %, fee 5 % on the Base Price = 90.00 + 5.00 = 95.00; on the Discounted Price = 90.00 + 4.50 = 94.50.
Carton above: cost 24 × 0.60 = 14.40 + 0.50 + 20 % of 14.40 (2.88) = 17.78 per carton.
Feature 4

Quotations priced per carton, per dozen or per unit

The engine looks at the line’s Packaging and UoM and converts the rule price into the line Unit Price. The optional Price per Package column shows the carton price (17.78). Lines without a packaging rule fall back to the next applicable rule: the 30 loose bottles use the category formula (0.90).

Export Europe: EUR pricelist based on Wholesale (−5 %, +2 % fee on the discounted price). The packaging is passed on to the base pricelist. Odoo 18 keeps the unit price at full precision, so carton subtotals are exact.
Feature 5

Quantities that follow the unit and the packaging

Selecting Carton of 24 on a line of 30 units rounds the quantity up to 48 (2 cartons) and tells the salesperson. Changing the UoM converts the quantity, so 24 Units become 2 Dozens. Both behaviours are company settings, enabled by default.

Feature 6

Bulk review and editing of all rules

Pricelist Rules is an editable grid over all pricelists, with multi-edit, sorting, filters (Unit of Measure Rules, Packaging Rules), grouping by Sold As and export. Only the cells that apply to each rule are shown; formula columns (including Odoo 18’s Markup) are optional columns.

Feature 7

Four Excel templates, mapped automatically

Download them from the gear menu ⚙ › Import records of Pricelist Rules or Pricelists; each one appears with its own name. Every workbook has drop-down lists for the choice columns, a note on each header and an Instructions sheet in English and Arabic explaining every column and every example row. The files are also in the module folder excel_templates/.

Which rule wins

Odoo’s scope order is kept. Inside the same scope, targeted rules come first, then the highest minimum quantity.

1. Scope (standard Odoo)
Product Variant most specific
Product then the product template
Product Category then the category
All Products least specific
2. Inside a scope (this module)
Packaging line uses that packaging
Unit of Measure line is sold in that UoM
Any Unit standard rule
Example: a Product rule “Carton of 24 = 25.00” wins over a Product rule “1.20 per unit” for carton lines, whatever the creation order. A Product rule without condition still wins over a Category rule per dozen, as in standard Odoo.

Point of Sale companion app

The Odoo 18 Point of Sale computes prices in the browser with its own copy of the pricelist logic, so it needs a small companion: Advanced Sales Pricelist – Point of Sale bridge, published as a separate app.

Installation

  • Technical name sales_pricelist_pos_advanced; depends on sales_pricelist_advanced and point_of_sale.
  • Put it in your addons with this app: Odoo installs it automatically as soon as Point of Sale is installed, in any order.
  • No Point of Sale? It stays uninstalled; this app never installs Point of Sale.
  • No menu and no setting to configure.

What it does

  • Adds Extra Fee (%) to the POS price engine, with the same formula order as the backend.
  • Keeps rules with a Packaging or Unit of Measure condition out of the POS, which sells in the product’s unit only: a box price is never charged on one unit.
  • Packaging and UoM prices stay available on quotations and sales orders.
  • To give the box price at the till, use a rule without packaging and with Min. Quantity = box size.
Members pricelist in the POS: Arabica Coffee (Sales Price 100.00, Cost 50.00), rule Cost + 10 % from 5 units: 5 scans = 5 × 55.00 = 275.00.

Built for real-world corner cases

Each case below is covered by an automated test.

Carton line entered in Dozens
The carton price is converted to the line UoM: 216.00 per carton = 108.00 per dozen, subtotal unchanged.
Multi-currency and chained pricelists
Fixed package prices stay in the pricelist currency; formulas convert the base price. The packaging is passed on to “Other Pricelist” rules.
Discounts shown the Odoo 18 way
With the Discounts setting on, a Discount rule on a carton or a dozen shows its % on the package list price; fixed and formula rules give a net price, and a mark-up is never shown as a negative discount.
Prices that don’t divide evenly
200.00 per carton of 24 = 8.333333 per unit: Odoo 18 keeps the unit price at full precision, so the subtotal is exactly 200.00 even with 2 decimals.
Delivered or invoiced lines
Quantities are never converted or rounded on lines already delivered or invoiced, and never converted to zero.
Point of Sale
With the companion app (installed automatically with Point of Sale), UoM/packaging rules stay out of the POS and the percentage fee is added to POS prices.
Wrong configuration
A packaging of another product, a UoM of another category, UoM and packaging together, or a packaging on a category rule are rejected with a clear message.
Minimum margin alone
Odoo 18 still rejects a minimum margin without a maximum; the module allows it, matching what the price engine already does.
Deleted packaging, API and imports
Deleting a packaging removes its rules; prices are available from code with packaging=; standard pricing is unchanged when no condition is used.
